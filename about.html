About Skymind

We build automation that gives time back

Skymind Automation is a small team of engineers and automation specialists who believe most businesses are drowning in repetitive work that software should be handling instead.

Our Story

Started by people tired of watching great teams do robotic work

Skymind Automation was founded on a simple observation: the businesses we worked with weren't short on talent — they were short on time, because so much of it went into copy-pasting data, chasing status updates, and manually stitching tools together.

We started building small automations to fix our own workflows. Word spread, and Skymind became a dedicated practice helping other businesses do the same — using workflow automation platforms and, increasingly, AI agents that can handle judgment-based work, not just repetitive clicks.

Today we work with ecommerce brands, clinics, real estate teams, and professional services firms to design automation systems that actually fit how they operate.

Our Process

How we take you from audit to live system

01

Discovery & Audit

We interview your team and map current workflows to identify the highest-leverage automation opportunities — free of charge.

02

Proposal & Scope

You get a clear, fixed-scope plan: what we'll build, the tools involved, timeline, and expected impact.

03

Build

We design and build the automation or AI agent in your environment, testing continuously against real data.

04

Test & Refine

We run the system in parallel with your existing process until it's proven reliable, then cut over.

05

Launch & Support

We train your team, document the system, and stay on for ongoing monitoring and improvements.
